ℹ️ Safe Withdrawal Rate

The Safe Withdrawal Rate (SWR) is the maximum percentage of your portfolio you can withdraw in your first year of retirement without running out of money.

How we calculate it: We run an aggressive binary search through 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations, hunting for the absolute highest withdrawal rate that still maintains a ≥95% success rate (portfolio > $0) after 30 years.

This rate automatically adjusts based on your configuration, tracking your chosen inflation rate over time, and simulating the exact risk tolerance volatility you picked in your settings!

The projected dollar amounts shown in the chart below apply this safety rate to your median (50th percentile) projected portfolio value.
